# Surface: landing page (web/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: hackathon judges; action: open the viewer and solve a plan. Proof: a real solved plan in 3D,
real benchmark numbers. Constraints: offline, real data only, no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the solved floor plan is the hero object. A real gallery plan, extruded in 3D with glowing cable loops and
hubs, floats centre-stage and tilts toward the cursor; the page refuses the category's split hero with a screenshot.
OWN-WORLD: deep night gradients that change per plan (abyss teal for 1 hub, midnight indigo for 2, deep plum for 3+),
copper accent (the cable's own metal, #f2b680), glass navigation pill, Galada script headlines, Manrope nav, Inter body,
hub colours as light sources.
STORY: the visitor sees fewer hubs and closed loops working on a real flat, switches between 1-, 2- and 3-hub plans,
believes the numbers, and clicks "Solve a plan".
FIRST VIEWPORT: glass header (wordmark, nav pill, "Open the viewer"); left: "Fewer hubs." / "Closed loops." script
headline, one-paragraph description, dark pill CTA with a copper disc (the reference site's button, translated), proof badge; centre: full-bleed 3D plan; right: three plan cards with
arrows above "No single point" / "of failure"; bottom centre: a caption pill naming the shown plan (Valid · plan · hubs · cost) that opens it in the viewer. Phone: headline, plan, copy, cards; nav pill as a scrolling row; no floaters.
FORM: pinned by the user's reference site (soda hero structure), translated to 8ball. Signature interaction: choosing a
plan card spins the plan 720 degrees with motion blur, swaps the plan at the peak, morphs the background, and the
floating devices implode to the centre and burst back out. Direction pinned by the user's brief; roll recorded for reference only. seed:2413df9a58c9864b
