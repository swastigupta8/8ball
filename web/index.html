Fewer hubs. Shorter cables. No single point of failure.

8ball reads a floor plan, places the fewest network hubs it can, and wires every device onto a closed cable loop through the doorways. If one cable is cut, every device is still reachable the other way round.

How to use it

Three steps, all in the viewer.

1

Pick a plan

Type a ResPlan plan ID and press Load, or upload your own floor plan as a .json file.

2

Solve

Press Solve. Hubs are placed and every cable loop is routed in seconds, then checked against every rule.

3

Explore and download

See each hub's path, the cost heatmap and what happens when a cable breaks. Download the solution or an image.

What makes it different

Built to be checked, not just believed.

Resilience

Break the cable

Cut any cable and watch the devices: our loops lose none, while a branching layout loses everything past the cut.

Transparency

Hub cost heatmap

Every spot in a zone is coloured by what the hub would cost there. The chosen hub always sits at the cheapest spot.

Trust

Independent checker

A second program, written separately, re-checks every rule and recomputes every number. They have never disagreed.

Scale

Optimal at any size

An exact search finds the fewest hubs, and a CP-SAT model confirms the choice and scales to whole floors in a fraction of a second.
